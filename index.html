Core Concept 1

Hypothesis & Prediction

A tentative, testable statement predicting the relationship between consumer variables (e.g., "If ad color is blue, then purchase intent increases").

Predictive Rigor 96%
Core Concept 2

Variables: IV, DV & Controlled

Independent (IV): Manipulated cause.
Dependent (DV): Measured effect.
Controlled: Kept constant to avoid confounding.

Experimental Control 92%
Core Concept 3

Operational Definitions

Clear, precise instructions on how abstract marketing concepts (e.g., "brand loyalty", "customer satisfaction") are measured in numbers or categories.

Measurability 90%
Core Concept 4

Population & Sample

Population: The entire target group (e.g., all Guatemalan coffee consumers).
Sample: The subset selected to participate in the study.

Generalizability 89%
Core Concept 5

Basic Sampling Methods

1. Random Sampling: Every population member has an equal chance of selection.
2. Convenience Sampling: Selecting readily available participants (e.g., local mall shoppers).
3. Purposive Sampling: Hand-picking experts or specific consumer profiles based on study criteria.
Sampling Validity 95%
